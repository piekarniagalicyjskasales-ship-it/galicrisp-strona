About GaliCrisp, the brand of Piekarnia Galicyjska

We bring together years of bakery expertise and a mindset focused on the shelf of tomorrow.

More than 30 years of baking

Behind our products are years of experience, evolving technology and cooperation with partners across many markets. GaliCrisp is the brand under which Piekarnia Galicyjska in Jeżowe bakes crispbread, crackers and bakery snacks.

Who we bake for

We work with food brands, retailers, distributors and foodservice partners in Poland and international markets. We adapt products, formats and cooperation models to the needs of each market and partner. Different businesses have different needs, and flexibility is part of how we work.

Five categories

Crispbread, flatbread crisps, crackers, bakery snacks and savoury biscuits. Together 40 catalogue items and more than 20 export markets.

  • 30+years of baking experience
  • 20+export markets
  • 5product categories, 40 catalogue items
  • A+BRCGS audit grade
