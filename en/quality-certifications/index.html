Quality and certificates: BRCGS A+, IFS Food Higher Level, BIO

For retailers, food safety is the ticket to the shelf. Our plant in Jeżowe meets the standards European buyers ask for.

BRCGSA+

BRCGS Global Standard for Food Safety, grade A+

One of the key GFSI recognised food safety standards. Grade A+ means an unannounced audit with the top result.

IFS Food, Higher Level

A standard required by many retailers in Germany, France and Italy. Higher Level is the top IFS certification level.

Organic certificate (PL-EKO-07)

Our EU organic certification lets us produce BIO lines, including under retailer brands.

What we share with partners

Certificate copies, product specifications, allergen and nutrition declarations, and logistics data for every line. Write to us and we will send the full set to your quality team.
