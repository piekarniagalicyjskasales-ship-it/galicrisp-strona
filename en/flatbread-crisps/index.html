Flatbread crisps in 6 flavours

Long, thin flatbread crisps in 130 g packs. Six flavours in one consistent range, from 3 Seeds with sea salt to Sesame and Beetroot.

  • Flatbread Crisps 130 g

Flatbread crisps are thin, flat crispbread in a 130 g pack. They are lighter in texture than a classic slice and work as a snack straight from the pack, with cheese, spreads and dips.

The range is available under the GaliCrisp brand and as private label. A one-sided display carton holds 11 packs and a pallet 2376 units, which makes listing and promotions simple.

Frequently asked questions

How are flatbread crisps different from crispbread?

They are thinner, longer and lighter than a classic slice, and work better as a snack and on the cheese board.

How many flavours are there?

Six: 3 Seeds and Sea Salt, Sesame and Beetroot, Tomato and Basil, Cheese and Tomato, Cheese and Onion, and Triple Cheese.
